# Golub & Van Loan — fourth-edition bibliography

## Authoritative source

Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan publish the bibliography for the fourth edition of *Matrix Computations* as a separate supplement:

- **G.H. Golub and C.F. Van Loan, *Matrix Computations (4th Edition): The Bibliography*, 1 December 2012, 66 pages.**
- Cornell-hosted copy: https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/blogs.cornell.edu/dist/c/9924/files/2021/11/GVL4_Bib.pdf

The Hopkins fourth edition deliberately moved the master bibliography out of the printed volume because of its size. This 66-page file, not an older second- or third-edition bibliography scraped elsewhere, is the canonical source for the fourth-edition reference audit.

## Status

**Source recovered; full normalization/indexing in progress.**

The supplement is much larger than Trefethen–Bau's ten-page bibliography. It will be processed as bibliographic data, not by selecting only famous names. The rules are:

1. preserve every citation;
2. preserve all coauthors rather than collapsing to `et al.`;
3. build a unique contributor index for `thanks.md`;
4. keep books, papers, reports, software, and proceedings distinguishable;
5. attach chapter/topic cross-links later, so the bibliography becomes usable rather than merely a 66-page wall of citations;
6. retain the separate public PDF as provenance.

## First verified entries

The opening of the official bibliography begins with work by the following authors and collaborators, among others:

- J. O. Aasen — symmetric-to-tridiagonal reduction.
- N. N. Abdelmalek — roundoff analysis of Gram–Schmidt and least squares.
- P.-A. Absil, R. Mahony, R. Sepulchre, and P. Van Dooren — invariant-subspace iterations.
- E. Acar, D. M. Dunlavy, T. G. Kolda, and M. Mørup — tensor decompositions/factorizations.
- D. Achlioptas and F. McSherry — low-rank approximation.
- G. E. Adams, A. W. Bojanczyk, and F. T. Luk — product SVD computation.
- L. Adams and H. Jordan — iterative methods/SOR.
- R. C. Agarwal, F. G. Gustavson, and M. Zubair — parallel matrix multiplication.
- K. Ahlander and H. Munthe-Kaas — generalized Fourier transforms in numerical linear algebra.
- R. Alam and S. Bora — stable eigendecompositions.
- S. T. Alexander, C. T. Pan, and R. J. Plemmons — recursive least squares and hyperbolic rotations.
- P. Alfeld and D. J. Eyre — spline/sparse rectangular-system work.
- A. H. Al-Mohy and N. J. Higham — matrix exponential algorithms and conditioning.
- P. R. Amestoy, T. A. Davis, and I. S. Duff — sparse ordering.
- G. S. Ammar, W. B. Gragg, and L. Reichel — orthogonal-matrix eigenproblems.
- P. Amodio and F. Mazzia — block-tridiagonal systems and LU error analysis.
- A. A. Anda and H. Park — plane rotations with dynamic scaling.
- B. S. Andersen, J. Wasniewski, and F. G. Gustavson — recursive Cholesky factorization.
- E. Anderson, Z. Bai, C. Bischof, S. Blackford, J. Demmel, J. Dongarra, J. Du Croz, A. Greenbaum, S. Hammarling, A. McKenney, and D. Sorensen — *LAPACK Users' Guide*, 3rd ed.
- W. E. Arnoldi — minimized iteration for the matrix eigenvalue problem.
- U. Ascher and C. Greif — *A First Course in Numerical Methods*.
- O. Axelsson and collaborators — iterative methods and preconditioning.

This opening slice is intentionally labeled as a slice. The 66-page source, not this list, determines completeness until the entire supplement has been normalized.
