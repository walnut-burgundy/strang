## Material already recoverable from the official site

The table of contents and errata establish the book's method-level ancestry and spelling for many named methods: Taylor, Newton, Cholesky, Neumann, Gershgorin, Schur, Francis, Hermite, Chebyshev, Fourier, Richardson, Gauss, Romberg, Sard, Bernoulli, Euler–Maclaurin, Runge–Kutta, Galerkin, Ritz, Nelder–Mead, and Pareto, among others.

These names are **leads, not a substitute for the bibliography**. Publication-level citations will be taken from the book when the back matter is available.
