Source: the bibliography in the free-access back matter of Lloyd N. Trefethen and David Bau III, *Numerical Linear Algebra* (SIAM, 1997), pp. 343–352.

Authoritative back matter: https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/1.9780898719574.bm

This file records bibliographic facts rather than reproducing the book's explanatory prose. Obvious font-extraction damage in the publisher PDF has been normalized. Three entries printed as `et al.` have been expanded from authoritative records so that contributors are not hidden.

## Audit notes

- The SIAM PDF's old embedded font causes some years and page numbers to extract as symbols. The entries above were normalized against SIAM's indexed bibliography and, where necessary, authoritative publisher/author records.
- The Trefethen–Bau key `[DoSo88]` points to a paper published in 1987; the publication year above follows the actual journal record rather than silently changing the book's citation key.
- Bruaset's book is 1995; some machine-readable secondary snippets misreport 1992.
- The full bibliography has now been transcribed. A separate acknowledgments/notes pass is still required because people credited in prose need not appear in the bibliography.
