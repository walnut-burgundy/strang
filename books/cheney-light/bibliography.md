## Status

**Bibliography identified; full normalization pending.**

Catalog and publisher records place the bibliography at **pp. 327–354** and describe it as containing **almost 600 items**. This is therefore a major source corpus in its own right, not a short list to reconstruct from secondary citations.

AMS exposes an end-matter PDF (`gsm101-endmatter.pdf`), but the PDF retrieval route used during this audit currently returns HTTP 403. Until the authoritative pages can be read reliably, this repository should preserve the source/pages and avoid replacing the bibliography with an incomplete web-derived list.

## Copyright/source rule

AMS end matter states that systematic copying or republication requires a license. Bibliographic facts can be normalized into this repository, but the book's protected exposition should not be mirrored.
