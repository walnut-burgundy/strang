## Credit rule

When a chapter note uses a named theorem, algorithm, factorization, historical result, or cited source, prefer a real bibliographic entry to an unexplained surname. When the book itself credits someone in its acknowledgments, preserve that credit here even if the person did not author a formally cited publication.

The goal is not ceremonial name accumulation. It is to keep the dependency graph of ideas visible.
