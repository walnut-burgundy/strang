# Thanks

This repository exists because numerical methods are a cumulative craft. The names below are not decorative: they mark people whose books, algorithms, theorems, historical work, teaching, or cited research are already visible in the source trail collected here.

Thanks to **Gilbert Strang**, for making linear algebra unusually readable and for treating it as something people actually use.

Thanks to the authors of the core books: **Forman S. Acton; Gene H. Golub; Charles F. Van Loan; David Kincaid; Ward Cheney; Lloyd N. Trefethen; David Bau III**.
