# Thanks

This repository exists because numerical methods are a cumulative craft. The names below are not decorative: they mark people whose books, algorithms, theorems, historical work, teaching, or cited research are already visible in the source trail collected here.

Thanks to **Gilbert Strang**, for making linear algebra unusually readable and for treating it as something people actually use.

Thanks to the authors of the core books: **Forman S. Acton; Gene H. Golub; Charles F. Van Loan; David Kincaid; Ward Cheney; Will Light; Lloyd N. Trefethen; David Bau III**.

## Completed per-book thank-you ledgers

- **Trefethen & Bau, *Numerical Linear Algebra* (1997):** [`books/trefethen-bau/thanks.md`](books/trefethen-bau/thanks.md) explicitly names every author/editor/translator represented in the book's bibliography, expands the bibliography's `et al.` entries, and separately names every recoverable person in the acknowledgments and dedication. The provenance details are in [`books/trefethen-bau/acknowledgments.md`](books/trefethen-bau/acknowledgments.md).

The other core books remain under bibliography/acknowledgment audit. Their names will be added in their own ledgers rather than being folded into a vague collective sentence.
